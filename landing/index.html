How it works

A full call, handled end-to-end

From the moment you dial to the confirmation — Mike handles it autonomously, escalating to a human only when confidence drops below threshold.

01
📞

You call

Vapi answers the call, converts your speech to text in real time using Deepgram.

02
🧠

Intent classified

Claude classifies your intent — schedule, resolve, or escalate — with a confidence score.

03
🔧

Tools called

Real tools check availability, book appointments, and verify insurance against live data.

04
✅

Confirmed

Claude generates a natural voice reply. Vapi speaks it back. Call complete.
